How to Use miTmood 🌸

• 1. Select Your Mood: Choose Happy (red), Neutral (yellow), or Sad/Tired (purple) from the top bar.


• 2. Color the Flower: Tap the numbered petal matching today's date to fill it with your mood color.


• 3. Erase / Edit: Tap an already colored petal again to clear it, or use the menu's eraser mode.


• 4. Zoom: Use the left slider (+ / -) to zoom in on petals.


• 5. Interactive Garden: Tap the butterflies, moths, or the golden bee to startle them into flight!

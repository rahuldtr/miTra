How to Bloom miTPetal 🌸

• 1. Pick Your Mood: Select Happy (Joyful Red), Neutral (Sunny Yellow), or Sad/Tired (Restful Purple).


• 2. Color Your Petal: Tap today's number on the flower to bloom it with your color.


• 3. Erase / Edit: Tap an already colored petal again to reset it, or turn on eraser mode in the menu.


• 4. Zoom: Use the left slider (+ / -) to zoom into petals smoothly.


• 5. Interactive Garden: Tap the butterflies, moths, or the sparkling bee to watch them fly through the garden air!

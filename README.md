We count the Sundays.🌊 The Story Behind the CounterAsk anyone on land how long until they return home, and they will tell you in dates and weeks.Ask a seafarer out in the middle of the ocean, rolling in heavy swells with thousands of miles of blue in every direction, and the answer is always different:"Five more Biriyanis, Chief." > "Halfway done—seven Biriyanis served, seven left to go."For generations across merchant ships, the Sunday Biriyani has been far more than just a meal. It is a shared ritual in the mess room. It is the moment engine grease and bridge watch fatigue pause for an hour. It is the steady heartbeat of a shipboard contract—a weekly reminder that no matter how long the voyage, how rough the passage, or how deep the homesickness runs, you are one Sunday closer to the gangway, one Sunday closer to family, and one Sunday closer to home.miTra Biriyani Counter (miTra02) was born out of that shared heartbeat. It transforms cold contract calendars into a warm, visual journey that honors the sailor’s tradition of counting time in plates of Sunday Biriyani.🧭 What miTra Biriyani Counter DoesBuilt as a lightweight, 100% offline single-file portal, this app needs no satellite Wi-Fi, external servers, or installations.

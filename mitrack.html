miTrack Distance: Mitra Formula & Specifications Manual

Mitra Formula & Mathematical Derivations

Comprehensive governing equations implemented in the voyage optimization core.

1. Theoretical Engine Speed & Apparent Slip

V_engine = (RPM × Pitch [m] × 60) / 1852 [knots]

S_apparent = ((V_engine - V_obs) / V_engine) × 100 [%]

2. SFOC & Brake Power Thermal Balance

SFOC = (FOC_daily × 10^6) / (P_B × 24) [g/kWh]

Directly relates bunker metric tonnage to effective 2-stroke shaft power output and thermal brake efficiency.

3. Relative Wind & Hydrodynamic Resistance

V_app = √[(V_wind · cos(θ_rel) + V_sog)² + (V_wind · sin(θ_rel))²]

ΔV_wave = 0.24 · (H_s)^1.85 · max(0, cos(θ_wave))

4. Admiralty Cubic Power Scaling Law

FOC_eff = FOC_rated × (V_eff / V_rated)³ [MT/day]

Governs fuel savings when applying slow-steaming and speed optimization modes.

5. IMO Operational Carbon Intensity Indicator (CII)

CII_attained = (Total_CO₂_Grams) / (Capacity_DWT × Total_Distance_NM)

CII_ref = a · DWT^(-c) (IMO Resolution MEPC.337(76))

1. Kinematic Propeller Dynamics & Apparent Slip
Theoretical distance advanced per hour by the propeller with zero hydrodynamic slip vs observed Speed Over Ground (SOG):
Advance Speed: V_eng = (RPM × Pitch [m] × 60) / 1852 [knots]
Apparent Slip: S_apparent = [(V_eng - V_obs) / V_eng] × 100 [%]
• Active: RPM = 74.0 | Pitch = 5.70 m • Step 1: Advance Advance = 74.0 × 5.70 × 60 = 25,308 m/hr • Step 2: V_eng = 25,308 ÷ 1852 = 13.67 knots • Step 3: At observed V_obs = 12.80 kn ➔ Slip = ((13.67 - 12.80) ÷ 13.67) × 100 = +6.36%
2. Significant Wave Added Resistance (Hs Formulation)
Semi-quadratic head-sea reflection penalty applied as speed loss based on wave spectral energy:
Wave Loss: ΔV_wave = 0.24 · (H_s)^1.85 · max(0, cos(θ_rel_wave)) [knots]
Current Drift: V_drift = V_current · cos(Dir_current - Course_ship) [knots]
• Step 1: Significant Wave Hs = 1.50 m, Rel Angle θ = 180° (Head Sea) • Step 2: Energy Exponent: (1.50)^1.85 ≈ 2.117 • Step 3: Head-Sea Drag Loss = 0.24 × 2.117 × cos(0°) = -0.51 knots • Current Projection: 1.2 kn @ 210° on Heading 210° = +1.20 kn aiding ground speed
3. Relative Wind Vector Resolution (Bridge Triangle)
True Wind vectors (u, v) combined with vessel forward velocity vector to derive apparent wind velocity over bridge deck:
u_rel = (-V_true · sin(TWD)) - (V_sog · sin(HDG))
v_rel = (-V_true · cos(TWD)) - (V_sog · cos(HDG))
Apparent Speed: V_app = √(u_rel² + v_rel²) | Dir: atan2(-u_rel, -v_rel)
• True Wind: 15.0 kn @ 280°T | Ship: 13.4 kn on Course 090°T • Cartesian u = -15.0·sin(280°) - 13.4·sin(90°) = +14.77 - 13.40 = +1.37 kn • Cartesian v = -15.0·cos(280°) - 13.4·cos(90°) = -2.60 - 0.00 = -2.60 kn • Resulting Relative Wind: 2.9 kn @ 152° Port | Wind Drag ΔV_wind = -0.05 kn
4. Specific Fuel Oil Consumption (SFOC) & Engine Brake Power
Thermodynamic brake specific consumption converting metric tonnage burned into shaft kilowatt-hours:
Engine Brake Power: P_B = P_MCR × (RPM / RPM_MCR)³ [kW]
SFOC: SFOC = (FOC_daily × 10⁶) / (P_B × 24) [g/kWh]
• Baseline 2-Stroke Slow Speed Diesel MCR: 11,500 kW @ 78.0 RPM • Service Operating Point: 74.0 RPM ➔ Power Ratio: (74/78)³ = 0.854 • Service Brake Power: P_B = 11,500 kW × 0.854 ≈ 9,820 kW • SFOC = (24.0 MT × 10⁶) ÷ (9,820 kW × 24 hrs) = 101.8 g/kWh (Propulsion Pure)

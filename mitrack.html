1.1 Theoretical Principles & Governing Equations
In marine engineering and naval architecture, a screw propeller operating in a fluid medium advances a theoretical distance equal to its face pitch per revolution. Because water is a dynamic fluid subject to boundary layer displacement and wake streams, the observed speed over ground ($V_{\text{obs}}$) deviates from the pitch advance speed ($V_{\text{eng}}$). This discrepancy is quantified as apparent slip.
Advance Speed: V_eng = (N [RPM] × P [m] × 60) / 1852 [knots]
Apparent Slip: S_apparent = [(V_eng - V_obs) / V_eng] × 100 [%]
True Slip (Taylor Wake): S_true = [(V_eng - V_a) / V_eng] × 100 [%]  |  V_a = V_obs / (1 - w)

1.3 Bridge Wind Vector Triangle & Aerodynamic Drag
Vector Proof: The apparent wind experienced over the bridge deck ($\vec{V}_{\text{app}}$) is the vector difference between true wind velocity ($\vec{V}_{\text{true}}$) and ship velocity over ground ($\vec{V}_{\text{ship}}$). Resolving into Cartesian components ($u, v$) allows precise aerodynamic superstructure drag calculations.
u_rel = (-V_true · sin(TWD)) - (V_sog · sin(HDG))
v_rel = (-V_true · cos(TWD)) - (V_sog · cos(HDG))
Apparent Speed: V_app = √(u_rel² + v_rel²) | Aerodynamic Drag: ΔV_wind = 0.0032 · (V_app)^1.62

1.4 Specific Fuel Oil Consumption (SFOC) & Thermal Efficiency
Marine slow-speed two-stroke diesel engines convert chemical fuel energy into mechanical shaft power. Specific Fuel Oil Consumption (SFOC) measures mass flow rate per unit of brake power output, directly reflecting thermal brake efficiency ($\eta_{\text{th}} = \frac{3600}{\text{SFOC} \times \text{LCV}}$).
Engine Brake Power: P_B = P_MCR × (RPM / RPM_MCR)³ [kW]
Specific Fuel Consumption: SFOC = (FOC_daily [MT] × 10⁶ [g]) / (P_B [kW] × 24 [h]) [g/kWh]

2.2 Stoichiometric Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$) Emissions (MEPC.308(73))
Under MARPOL Annex VI, carbon dioxide emissions are directly proportional to fuel consumption through a standardized carbon conversion factor ($C_F$). Complete combustion of hydrocarbon fuels yields stoichiometric carbon ratios based on carbon mass percentages.

LSMGO (87.44% Carbon): C_F = 3.2060 MT-CO₂ / MT-Fuel
Total Gross CO₂ = (Fuel_VLSFO × 3.1144) + (Fuel_LSMGO × 3.2060) [MT]

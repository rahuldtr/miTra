1. Theoretical Engine Speed (V_engine)
Forward distance moved per hour by the propeller with zero slip:
Formula: V_engine = (RPM × Pitch × 60) ÷ 1852 [kts]

2. Hydrodynamic Weather Penalties (ΔV_wave, ΔV_wind)
Quadratic head-sea added resistance from significant wave height & wind:
Formulas: ΔV_wave = 0.24·Hs^1.85·cos(θ) | ΔV_wind = 0.0032·V^1.62
--
3. Ocean Current Vector Resolution (V_drift)
Projected velocity along ship's navigational ground track:
Formula: V_current_proj = V_current × cos(Current_Dir - Ship_Course)
--
4. Observed Speed Over Ground (V_obs)
Net actual speed advanced over the ground:
Formula: V_obs = [V_eng × (1 - 0.05)] - ΔV_wave - ΔV_wind + V_current_proj
--
5. Apparent Propeller Slip (S_apparent)
Direct measurement of lost propulsion / water churning:
Formula: Slip % = [(V_engine - V_obs) ÷ V_engine] × 100
--
6. Slip-Induced Bunker Waste (FOC_waste)
Fuel consumed overcoming resistance above 5.0% calm clean water baseline:
Formula: Waste_MT = Days × FOC_eff × max(0, [Slip% - 5.0%] ÷ 100)
